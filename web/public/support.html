Verified Support

Help for the Verified app on iOS, Android and the web

Get help: open an issue at github.com/mattrickslauer/verified/issues. Tell us your device, app version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 business days.

What is Verified?

Verified records a moment as it happens (live GPS, motion, video and proof shots, hash-chained as they are captured). An on-device AI witness coaches you while you record, cloud AI judges the evidence afterwards, and the result is a public record anyone can check.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. You can browse and record as a guest. Sign in with Google to keep your Moments across devices.

Why does it ask for camera, microphone, location and motion? They are the evidence. Each one is used only while a Moment is recording or while you talk to the witness.

Who can see my Moment? Published Moments are public. You can delete any of your Moments from the Moment's page.

How do I delete my account? Open an issue at the link above titled "Delete my account". We delete your account and all its data within 30 days.

Why was my Moment "not verified"? The AI judge did not find enough evidence that what you set out to do really happened. Record a clearer clip or more proof shots and try again.
