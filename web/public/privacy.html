Privacy Policy

Verified · Last updated 27 November 2026

Verified records a moment (video, proof shots, GPS and motion) so it can be checked and published as a tamper-evident record. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Verified runs on iOS, Android and the web at verified-99e74.web.app.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, email and profile photo from your Google accountOnly if you sign in with Google. Guest mode signs you in anonymously and collects none of these.To identify your account and show your name on Moments you publish
Video clips, audio and proof photosOnly while you record a MomentThe evidence the Moment is built from
Precise location (GPS track)Only while a Moment is recording or you are talking to the witnessTo show where the Moment happened and check that it is real

We do not use advertising, ad identifiers or third-party analytics, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

How AI is used

Where possible, AI runs on your device (Apple Foundation Models on iOS, Gemini Nano on Android and in Chrome). When the device cannot handle a request, or for the final verdict on a Moment, the relevant text, images or video are sent to Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic and Vertex AI. That data is processed to produce the result. We do not use it to train models.

Who can see your Moments

A Moment you publish is public: anyone with the app or the link can see its video, photos, location and verdict. Moments you have not published stay private to your account. Clips you choose to save to Photos stay on your device.

Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check, Firebase AI Logic / Vertex AI) in the United States. If you buy verification credits on the web, the payment is handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your card details.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete any of your Moments in the app, which removes its record and media. To delete your whole account and everything linked to it, make a request through the support page below and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Verified is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
