A photo used to be proof. Today anyone can generate one in seconds, or just photograph a screen. So everything that runs on "pics or it didn't happen" (fitness challenges, fishing derbies, sponsor contests) now runs on trust.

So I didn't build a better photo checker. I built an intent and evidence factory that runs on your phone. You tell it what you're about to do, and it builds the proof while you do it.

It starts with intent. I just say it: "twenty push-ups at the top of the hill." An on-device model interviews me, right on the phone, no signal needed. It breaks my words into claims that can each be checked: a place, an action, a count. And for each claim it writes an evidence plan, the minimum a fair skeptic would need. Here, one continuous video of all twenty, and a GPS track that ends on the hill.

Then the factory runs. The phone records GPS, motion and video, and every five seconds it seals that into a chunk, chained to the last one with SHA-256. Change one byte later, and the chain breaks.

Here's the design decision I care most about. The on-device model checks every shot in under a second, and the image never leaves the phone. But it has exactly one power: it can say "retake." It can never say "verified." The small model coaches. It never judges.

While I record, a live AI witness asks for whatever is still missing, like "show me the view behind you," and my answer goes into the chain too. No signal? It all waits on the phone and syncs later.

Then the cloud takes over. Gemini watches the whole video, claim by claim, and a judge, a skeptic and a scorer read the frames. But AI only proposes. A Cloud Function re-checks the chain, the timing and the physics, and only that code can write the seal.

And a fake? Here's an AI-generated fish, filmed off my laptop. The skeptic spots the screen, and it's rejected. Faking one frame is easy. Faking a whole session is hard.

Push-ups are just one example. Any intent works: a run, a repair, a recipe, a song.

And it's one app on three platforms: iPhone, Android and Chrome, all through one Firebase AI Logic hybrid SDK. On-device when the phone has a model, cloud when it doesn't, and every answer shows you which one ran.

Proof is free. When the stakes are high, you can buy extra checks, but paying buys scrutiny, never a score.
