A photo used to be proof. Not anymore. Anyone can generate one in seconds, or photograph a screen. So everything that runs on "pics or it didn't happen" (fitness challenges, fishing derbies, sponsor contests) now runs on trust.

Verified is an intent and evidence factory that runs on your phone. You say what you're about to do, and it builds the proof while you do it.

It starts with intent. I say it out loud: "twenty push-ups at the top of the hill." An on-device model interviews me right on the phone, no signal needed, and breaks my words into claims that can each be checked: a place, an action, a count.

For every claim it writes an evidence plan, the minimum a fair skeptic would need: one continuous video of all twenty, and a GPS track that ends on the hill. It also sets a bar. Harder claims must clear a higher score.

Then the factory runs. The phone records GPS, motion and video, and every five seconds seals that data into a chunk, chained to the last with SHA-256. Change one byte later, and the chain breaks.

The on-device model checks each shot against the plan in under a second, and the image never leaves the phone. But it has one power: it can tell me to retake. It can never say verified. The small model coaches. It never judges.

A live AI witness asks for whatever is still missing. "Show me the view behind you." My answer is chained in too.

No signal? Everything waits in a local vault and syncs later. Then the cloud takes over. Gemini watches the whole video, claim by claim, while a judge, a skeptic and a scorer read the frames.

But AI only proposes. A Cloud Function re-checks the chain, the timing and the physics, compares the score with the bar, and only that code can write the seal.

Now a fake: an AI-generated trophy fish, filmed off a laptop. The skeptic sees the screen, there's no real motion, and it's rejected. Faking one frame is easy. Faking a whole session is hard.

Push-ups are just one example. The factory takes any intent: a run, a repair, a recipe, a song, a summit.

It runs on iOS, Android and Chrome through one Firebase AI Logic hybrid SDK: on-device when the phone has a model, cloud when it doesn't, and every answer shows which ran.

Proof is free. When stakes are high, owners can buy extra checks. Paying buys scrutiny, never a score.
